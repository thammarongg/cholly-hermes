Cholly Hermes

A personal AI assistant run by Thammarong Glomjai

Cholly Hermes is a self-hosted personal assistant built on the open-source Hermes Agent. It runs on its owner's own computer and acts only on its owner's own Google and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

With the owner's permission, it helps with routine work on the owner's own accounts, such as managing Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Docs, and managing videos, playlists and video details on the owner's YouTube channel.
