Privacy Policy

Cholly Hermes · Last updated 15 October 2026

Cholly Hermes ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted assistant operated by Thammarong Glomjai for his own use. It is not a public service. This policy explains what data the app accesses when its owner signs in with Google, and how that data is handled.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel. It does not collect data from any other person.

Data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes it through Google OAuth, the app may access:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the owner's channel information, videos, playlists and video metadata, and the ability to upload videos and update video details, playlists and thumbnails on the owner's channel.
  • Google Workspace: the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Contacts, to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

The app only requests the permissions needed for the tasks the owner uses it for.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform actions the owner asks the app to perform on the owner's own accounts. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to build profiles.

Storage

OAuth tokens and any data the app retrieves are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app does not operate a server and does not upload this data to any database run by the app.

Sharing

The app does not share Google or YouTube user data with third parties. To carry out a task, content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has configured for the agent; this happens only on the owner's instruction and only for that task.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page or the Google security settings page. Deleting the local token files removes the app's stored access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
